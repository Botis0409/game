Cercul

A game you play by reading.

Every sentence on screen is an instruction. The child reads it, then does it — flying a griffin over a meadow, then crawling as an ant through its own picnic. The words are the controls.

Play the demo Watch the video

Runs in the browser, nothing to install. Best on a phone held sideways. One level, about three minutes.

How it works

Read it. Then do it.

1

A sentence appears

Fly over the water.

Short sentences built from the most frequent words in English, with the words being learned lit up in gold.

2

The child does it

Find the tree by the water.

Tap where to go. The griffin flies there. A sentence that was understood, not just sounded out, moves the game forward.

3

The world is the reward

Go down to the ants.

No points, no timers. The level ends with the list of words the child just read. Reading more is the only way to see more.

Why words, not buttons

Half of everything ever written

The 100 most frequent words in English make up about half of all printed text. A child who recognises them instantly reads fluently; a child who still stops at the, down and into guesses, and falls behind in every subject.

Cercul is built on exactly those words. In the game the sentence is not a caption — it is the control. A child who cannot read it cannot play; a child who reads it, plays. The motivation to read is the same motivation every kid already has: to see what happens next.

The demo is the first level of a world. Each level adds a handful of words; the sentences grow with the child.

What this is, and what comes next

A prototype you can hold

This is a working build, not a mock-up: one complete level, real controls, running in a browser on a phone. It exists to show the idea in your hands.

  • More levels and more words — the next worlds reuse the griffin and the ant, so each level is mostly new sentences, not new engineering.
  • Reading before playing: a short word check that chooses which sentences a child gets next.
  • Native apps for iOS and Android (the same build already runs on both).
